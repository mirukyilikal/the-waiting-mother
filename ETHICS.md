# Ethics and Responsible AI — The Waiting Mother

## 1. Purpose

This document explains the ethical considerations involved in creating The Waiting Mother using AI-assisted tools.

The film tells a fictional story about maternal love in a rural Ethiopian setting.

## 2. Consent and Representation

The mother and daughter portrayed in the film are fictional AI-generated characters.

No real person's identity, likeness, or voice was intentionally used or imitated.

The narration uses a synthetic Amharic text-to-speech voice rather than a recording of a real person.

The film does not claim to document an actual family or real-life event.

## 3. Cultural Representation

The story is inspired by the importance of family relationships and maternal care.

The setting and characters are intended to represent a fictional Ethiopian rural environment.

Care was taken to avoid presenting the fictional story as a factual representation of every Ethiopian family or community.

## 4. AI Transparency

AI-assisted tools were used to create visual assets and video scenes.

Synthetic text-to-speech was used for Amharic narration.

An AI disclosure card is included at the end of the final film so viewers are aware of the AI-assisted production.

## 5. Copyright and Licensing

Background music was obtained from Pixabay.

The music is third-party material and is not an original composition created for this project.

The applicable licence and usage conditions for the music and all other third-party assets must be checked before public distribution.

AI-generated images, video clips, and narration should also be reviewed against the terms of the platforms used to create them.

## 6. Privacy and Data Protection

The project does not intentionally use personal information belonging to real individuals.

No private family recordings or personal voice recordings were intentionally included.

Project files and production logs should not contain passwords, private access tokens, or other sensitive information.

## 7. Potential Risks

AI-generated media can create unrealistic or misleading representations.

To reduce this risk:

- The story is clearly presented as fictional.
- The characters are not presented as real people.
- The final film includes an AI disclosure.
- Third-party music and assets are subject to licence checks.

## 8. Limitations

AI-generated visuals may contain inconsistencies in character appearance, clothing, movement, or background details.

Synthetic narration may not perfectly reproduce every nuance of natural Amharic speech.

These limitations should be considered when evaluating the film.

## 9. Ethical Commitment

The project aims to use AI responsibly, communicate transparently with viewers, respect privacy, and avoid misleading claims about real people or events.
