# Ethics and Responsible AI — The Waiting Mother

## 1. Purpose

This document explains the ethical considerations involved in creating *The Waiting Mother* using AI-assisted tools.

The film tells a fictional story about maternal love in a rural Ethiopian setting. Its purpose is to communicate the importance of family relationships, care, and emotional support while using AI tools responsibly.

## 2. Consent and Representation

The mother and daughter portrayed in the film are fictional AI-generated characters.

No real person's identity, likeness, or voice was intentionally used or imitated in the production.

The narration uses a synthetic Amharic text-to-speech voice rather than a recording of a real person.

The film does not claim to document an actual family, individual, or real-life event.

Because the characters are fictional, the project does not use real individuals as performers or subjects.

## 3. Cultural Representation

The story is inspired by the importance of family relationships and maternal care in everyday life.

The setting and characters are intended to represent a fictional Ethiopian rural environment.

Care was taken to avoid presenting the fictional story as a factual representation of every Ethiopian family, culture, or community.

The project recognizes that Ethiopian communities have diverse traditions, languages, lifestyles, and family experiences.

## 4. AI Transparency

AI-assisted tools were used to create visual assets, generate video scenes, and produce narration.

Synthetic text-to-speech was used for the Amharic narration.

An AI disclosure card is included at the end of the final film to inform viewers that AI-assisted tools were used in its production.

The project does not present AI-generated scenes or characters as authentic documentary footage.

## 5. Copyright and Licensing

Background music was obtained from Pixabay.

The music is third-party material and is not an original composition created specifically for this project.

The applicable licence and usage conditions for the music must be checked and retained before public distribution.

AI-generated images, video clips, and narration should also be reviewed against the terms and usage policies of the platforms used to create them.

Any third-party material included in the project should be used in accordance with its applicable licence.

## 6. Privacy and Data Protection

The project does not intentionally use personal information belonging to real individuals.

No private family recordings or personal voice recordings were intentionally included.

Project files and production logs should not contain passwords, private access tokens, personal credentials, or other sensitive information.

## 7. Potential Risks

AI-generated media can create unrealistic, misleading, or culturally inaccurate representations.

To reduce these risks:

- The story is clearly presented as fictional.
- The characters are not presented as real people.
- The final film includes an AI disclosure.
- Visual and audio outputs are reviewed before inclusion.
- Third-party music and assets are subject to licence checks.

## 8. Limitations

AI-generated visuals may contain inconsistencies in character appearance, clothing, movement, or background details.

Synthetic narration may not perfectly reproduce every nuance of natural Amharic pronunciation, expression, and delivery.

AI-generated content may also reflect limitations or biases in the tools used to produce it.

These limitations should be considered when evaluating the quality, authenticity, and cultural representation of the film.

## 9. Ethical Commitment

The project aims to use AI responsibly, communicate transparently with viewers, respect privacy, and avoid misleading claims about real people or events.

It also recognizes the importance of respecting cultural representation, intellectual property, and the limitations of AI-generated media.
