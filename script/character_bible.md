# THE WAITING MOTHER
## Character Bible

### Visual Style
- Realistic cinematic photography.
- Ethiopian rural village setting.
- Warm late-afternoon sunlight.
- Natural skin texture and realistic facial expressions.
- Emotionally authentic, respectful portrayal of rural Ethiopian life.
- No cartoon or exaggerated visual effects.

### Character 1: The Mother

- Age: Approximately 40–45.
- Appearance: Ethiopian woman with dark hair tied back.
- Clothing: Traditional white Ethiopian dress with a white netela-style shawl featuring colorful embroidered trim.
- Expression: Gentle, patient, warm, and loving.
- Personality: Caring, dignified, strong, and emotionally supportive.

**Continuity rules:**
- Keep the same face and hairstyle in every shot.
- Keep the same traditional white clothing and embroidered shawl.
- Do not change her apparent age.
- Her emotions should develop naturally from patient waiting to happiness and affection.

### Character 2: The Daughter

- Age: Approximately 9.
- Appearance: Ethiopian schoolgirl with dark braided hair.
- Clothing: Light-blue collared school shirt, navy school uniform, and dark navy backpack.
- Expression: Curious, innocent, cheerful, and affectionate.
- Personality: Energetic, respectful, and close to her mother.

**Continuity rules:**
- Keep the same face and hairstyle in every shot.
- Keep the same school uniform and backpack.
- Do not change her apparent age.
- Maintain consistent proportions and facial features.

### Environment

- Rural Ethiopian village.
- Modest but welcoming family home.
- Natural dirt road.
- Green plants and trees.
- Warm afternoon sunlight.
- Peaceful atmosphere.

### Global Visual Consistency Rules

1. The mother and daughter must look like the same two fictional characters in every shot.
2. Clothing, hairstyle, age, and facial features must remain consistent.
3. Avoid distorted faces, extra fingers, unnatural body proportions, and artificial-looking expressions.
4. Avoid stereotypes or portraying rural Ethiopian life as miserable.
5. Use realistic cinematic lighting and natural movements.
6. Maintain the emotional tone of a loving mother waiting for her daughter.
