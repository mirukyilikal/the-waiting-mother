# THE WAITING MOTHER
## Environment Bible

### Main Location
A peaceful rural Ethiopian village.

### Family Home
- Modest mud-and-stone exterior.
- Wooden door, preferably blue-green.
- Simple wooden fencing.
- Flowering plants near the entrance.
- Welcoming and well-maintained surroundings.

### Village Road
- Natural dirt road passing in front of the home.
- Trees and green plants along the road.
- Simple rural houses in the background.
- Distant green hills.

### Lighting
- Late-afternoon golden sunlight.
- Warm natural colors.
- Soft, realistic shadows.
- Consistent time-of-day appearance.

### Cinematography
- Realistic cinematic photography.
- Natural camera movement.
- Wide establishing shots for the village.
- Medium shots for character actions.
- Close-ups for emotional dialogue.
- Natural depth of field.

### Continuity Rules
1. Use the same family home throughout the film.
2. Keep the same village road and surrounding environment.
3. Maintain warm late-afternoon lighting.
4. Avoid sudden changes in weather or season.
5. Avoid modern city buildings and unrelated urban objects.
6. Keep the environment peaceful, welcoming, and authentic.
7. S01 must introduce the village without revealing the daughter early.
